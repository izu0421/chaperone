SLITRK2 ↔ GPR22 CONFIRMED_NOVEL VALIDATE low confidence ipTM 0.5 · pTM
Rationale
No prior record of this pair exists: STRING found no association, CellPhoneDB found no curated interaction, and PubMed returned 0 co-mentions. The pair is biologically plausible, since both are cell-membrane proteins per UniProt (SLITRK2 Q9H156 single-pass, GPR22 Q99680 7-TM orphan GPCR) and both are brain-expressed per HPA. The structural support is weak, however: ipTM is 0.5, exactly at the threshold; interface pLDDT is 63.4; the supplied p_twoway is 0.057; both proteins carry 2 annotated glycosylation sites whose position relative to the interface could not be checked; and the membrane side of the interface is unverified. This is a novel, plausible hypothesis, not a supported interaction.
PTM / glycosylation
UniProt marks both proteins as glycoproteins with 2 annotated glycosylation sites each. SLITRK2 additionally has 4 disulfide bonds and 1 modified residue and is a phosphoprotein. The PubMed PTM search returned 1 hit for SLITRK2 (PMID 27812321, on Slitrk missense mutations impairing trafficking and synapse formation) and 2 hits for GPR22, neither of which is about GPR22 glycosylation. Neither protein's function text describes a glycan- or lipid-mediated binding mechanism. Glycosylation sites on such proteins are typically extracellular, and the supplied interface is very large (buried surface area 6162 Å2, 1280 contacts), so overlap with a glycan site is possible. It could not be checked without per-residue interface data, so this is an unresolved caveat and not evidence of an artifact.

Rationale
The pair is novel (no STRING record, no CellPhoneDB hit, 0 PubMed co-mentions) and both are plasma-membrane proteins, so a cis interaction is topologically possible. However, L1CAM (P32004) carries 20 annotated glycosylation sites on its ectodomain and GPR61 (Q9BZJ8) has 1 N-glycosylation site on its extracellular N-terminus. The modelled interface is very large (buried surface area 5999 Å2, 1330 contacts) but only modestly confident (interface pLDDT 55.9, ipTM 0.61). An interface of that size on L1CAM very probably covers glycan-bearing surface that AF3 did not model, and may also involve GPR61 surface that is buried in the lipid bilayer; neither could be checked without a fold. Expression overlap in HPA is also weak.
PTM / glycosylation
UniProt annotates L1CAM (P32004) with the Glycoprotein and Phosphoprotein keywords, 20 glycosylation sites, 9 modified residues and 6 disulfide bonds. Given its single transmembrane helix, the glycosylation sites are expected to lie on the large extracellular Ig/FnIII ectodomain. UniProt annotates GPR61 (Q9BZJ8) as a Glycoprotein with 1 glycosylation site; PMID 29226084 ("N-glycosylation and expression in human tissues of the orphan GPR61 receptor") reports its N-glycosylation. The PubMed PTM search returned 85 hits for L1CAM and 2 for GPR61. An interface burying 5999 Å2 on a 20-site glycoprotein ectodomain is likely to overlap glycan positions, but the per-residue overlap was not checked because no fold could be run.
