Validation strategy: PLA (co-localization) (PLA with parallel +/- neuraminidase (sialidase) and PNGase F treatment arms to test glycan-dependence of the signal)
Why this method
Both CD69 and CLEC2B are membrane-anchored CTLD proteins co-expressed on the same immune cells (NK-cells, T-cells, innate lymphoid cells), so a direct cis/trans surface-proximity assay is appropriate rather than a ligand-receptor stimulation format. PLA in situ or on fixed cells will reveal whether the two proteins actually sit close enough to interact, and by adding glycosidase-treatment arms we can directly interrogate whether the observed proximity signal depends on CD69's carbohydrate-recognition activity or on CLEC2B's N-glycans, addressing the core PTM/glycan artifact concern head-on rather than just confirming co-localization.
Target tissue(s)
lymphoid tissue, bone marrow
Why these tissues (HPA)
Lymphoid tissue shows strong protein-level CLEC2B expression (624041.2 intensity units) alongside high CD69 RNA (88.3 nTPM), and bone marrow shows both CD69 (159.6 nTPM) and CLEC2B (106.6 nTPM) RNA expression — these are the only two tissues in the HPA data with quantifiable signal for both proteins, making them the best real overlap for tissue-section PLA.
Cell type(s)
NK-cells, T-cells, Innate lymphoid cells
Protocol notes
1) Use primary human NK-cells or T-cells (freshly isolated or short-term activated, since CD69 is an activation marker and may need TCR/cytokine stimulation to reach surface levels detected in single-cell data) fixed non-permeabilized to preserve surface glycans. 2) Apply validated anti-CD69 and anti-CLEC2B antibody pairs (different host species) for PLA probe conjugation; include a tissue section arm from lymphoid tissue (e.g., tonsil or lymph node) given the strong CLEC2B protein signal there. 3) Run parallel samples pretreated with neuraminidase (removes sialic acids) or PNGase F (removes N-glycans, targeting CLEC2B's 3 N-glycosylation sites) before PLA probing; quantify puncta/cell across conditions. 4) Include a companion cis-binding ELISA/flow cytometry assay with recombinant CD69-Fc and CLEC2B-Fc (glycosylated vs. enzymatically deglycosylated) to corroborate PLA findings biochemically.
Controls
Positive control: known glycan-dependent lectin pair (e.g., CD69-galectin-1 PLA, or Siglec-sialoglycan pair) to confirm glycosidase treatment is effective and doesn't abolish unrelated PLA signals nonspecifically. Negative controls: single-antibody-only PLA (no interaction expected), isotype control antibodies, and a cell type expressing only one of the two proteins (e.g., a CLEC2B-negative T-cell subset or CD69-negative resting cell) to establish background puncta rate.
Caveats
This pair is flagged LIKELY_ARTIFACT_PTM because no AF3 interface was actually modeled (fold_evidence_status=not_run), so there is no structural basis to argue the interface is PTM-free — the entire premise of a bare-protein CTLD-CTLD contact is unsupported. CD69's documented physiological binding mode is carbohydrate/sialic-acid-dependent, and CLEC2B carries 3 N-glycosylation sites, so a positive PLA signal that disappears after neuraminidase/PNGase F treatment would confirm the interaction is glycan-mediated rather than a genuine protein-protein interface, which would still be biologically interesting but mechanistically different from what AF3 implicitly assumes. Also note CD69 surface levels are activation-dependent and low/absent on resting cells, so stimulation conditions must be carefully controlled to ensure both proteins are actually present at the cell surface during the assay.

Rationale
Expression, compartment and novelty all support the pair (zero STRING/CellPhoneDB/PubMed evidence), but LGALS1 is a galectin whose documented mechanism is carbohydrate binding, and CSF2RA carries 11 UniProt-annotated extracellular glycosylation sites. Any real LGALS1–CSF2RA association is most likely lectin–glycan, a mode AF3's unglycosylated model cannot represent, so the specific protein–protein interface predicted is probably an artifact rather than the true contact. Skipped fold_complex because the mechanism-level lectin signal, not a single residue's location, is what invalidates the modeled interface.
PTM / glycosylation
UniProt LGALS1 FUNCTION is explicitly mechanism-defining: "Lectin that binds beta-galactoside and a wide array of complex carbohydrates" — galectin-1's real binding is glycan-mediated, which AF3's bare-sequence fold cannot represent at all. UniProt CSF2RA: keyword "Glycoprotein" with 11 annotated Glycosylation sites on its extracellular domain (plus 2 disulfides, 1 TM), i.e. exactly the heavily N-glycosylated surface a galectin would engage. pubmed_ptm_glycosylation for CSF2RA returned 12 hits, mostly unrelated topics, but the UniProt record alone is decisive.
